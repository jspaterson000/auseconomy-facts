# auseconomy facts pack

Research outputs of an independent model of the Australian economy and Commonwealth budget built from primary
data (PBO, Treasury, Finance, ABS, RBA, ATO, NDIA). Updated 2026-10-27. Maintained by Jacob Paterson.

- `facts_pack.md`: curated facts with sources + the live indicator snapshot. Fetch this.
- `assessment.md`: question-led assessment. `why.md`: driver trees. `findings.md`: budget deep-dive.
- `research/`: sourced research briefs. `notes/`: per-module method notes. `figures/`: charts. `grok/`: external critiques.

Cite as "auseconomy model (Jacob Paterson), <file>, <date>" plus the primary source named in the fact.
Numbers tagged [estimate] are the model's own calculations; [contested] means credible sources disagree.
