## 2. Decomposition and projections
- Year to Mar-2026: growth 392.7k (1.4%); NOM 292.1k (74%), natural increase 100.6k (26%). Year to Mar-2025: 414.9k.
- Budget 2026-27 population at 30 June: 27.614m (2024-25) → 29.372m (2029-30); growth ≈398k, 352k, then ~336k/yr. TFR 1.44 in 2025-26.
- 2025 Population Statement (Jan 2026, corrected Mar 2026): 31.5m by 2035-36 (150k below previous); long-run NOM 235k; TFR recovering to 1.62 by 2031-32; natural increase 99k (2025-26) → 119k (2035-36). Holding population at 30m needs NOM ≈32k by 2035-36.

## 3. Claimed effects
- Rents: RBA (Bulletin Jul 2025) 50k more people → private rents ≈+0.5% vs baseline, more when vacancies tight. Budget 2026-27: +55k NOM revision ≈ 22k extra homes at 2.5 persons/dwelling. JEBO (Oct 2026, paywalled, unverified): 1pp local population inflow → house rents +2.8%, unit rents +4.3% (local, doesn't scale nationally). Grattan (Apr 2025, unverified): permanent-visa cut → rents ≈2.5% lower after a decade. Direction uncontested; size is.
- Per-capita GDP: Jun-q 2026 GDP per capita 0.0% q/q, +0.7% y/y; Mar-q −0.1%. GDP +0.4% q, +2.1% y. "Per-capita recession" is arithmetic, not causal evidence.
- Inflation: RBA SMP Aug 2026: population "adds to both supply and demand… broadly neutral"; population revision lifted potential growth to "a bit over 2%".
- Wages: Breunig et al (ANU 2017), Dustmann (RBA conf 2019): no measurable effect on incumbent wages once skill mix controlled; evidence predates the 2022–24 surge.
- Fiscal: Treasury (Dec 2021) lifetime fiscal impact, 2018-19 permanent cohort: +$127k/person vs general population; primary skilled +$415k; humanitarian −$400k; parent visas −$394k; temporary migrants not covered. PBO costing of Coalition 2025 permanent-program cut (185k→140k): UCB −$3.4bn over 4 yrs (−309m, −625m, −1,040m, −1,412m), first-round only. Coalition 2026 plan "budget neutral" with no modelling released.
- Infrastructure: ~$100k public infrastructure per extra resident is advocacy (SPA/van Onselen), no transparent method; no recent official estimate.

## 4. Policy settings (late 2026)
- Labor: permanent program 185k 2026-27 (132,240 skill / 52,460 family); NOM targets 245k (26-27), 225k (27-28); international student planning level 295k for 2027 (prioritisation, not hard cap); WHM 2nd/3rd-year capped 45k/5k.
- Coalition (Taylor, 6 Oct 2026): NOM 100k for two years, then 130k, 160k, then tied to housing completions; student commencements capped 240k; most temporary graduate visas abolished; humanitarian 20k→10k; bridging visas ~414k→20k; costings promised nearer 2028 election.
- One Nation: cut 750k temporary visas over 3 yrs (~−25k NOM/yr), then ceiling 130k; students ~100k/yr.
- Greens: no cut; humanitarian 50k.
- Newspoll 12 Oct 2026: ON 28, ALP 29, Coalition 20 (secondary).
