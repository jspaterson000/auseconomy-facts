# Driver trees: energy prices; Commonwealth spending growth outside NDIS (agent output, 2026-10-26)
Method for spending: growth 2019-20→2025-26 split into price (CPI +25.8%), demographics (pop +8.9%; 80+ +24.2%), residual = policy + wages (approximate). [mem] = from memory, not re-checked (web limit hit).

## A. Energy
0. Outcome (ABS CPI): 2019 avg → Jun-q 2026: gas +44.1%, electricity +26.4%, all groups +28.0% → electricity ≈flat real, gas ≈+13% real. Gas jump mostly Jun-2022→Mar-2023. Electricity index fell to 74.8 (Dec-2024) on rebates, then +22.4% yr to Jun-2026 as they ended.
1. Wholesale (main 2022–24 shock) [H]: 2022 global coal/gas spike (Ukraine), flooded mines, coal unit outages, cold winter; NEM spot avg $264/MWh, gas $28.40/GJ (Q2 2022); avg generation price $189/MWh (2022) → $104 (2025). Callide C4 (May 2021), ageing fleet; ~90% of coal retires by 2035; Eraring extended to Aug 2027. Q3 2026 avg $63/MWh (−27% y/y) as batteries flatten evening peaks.
2. Networks [H]: 39–54% of DMO. RAB ≈$123b ($28.3b transmission, $94.9b distribution); grew ≈8%/yr 2006–13 (state reliability standards, "gold-plating"), ≈1%/yr since 2014; network revenue −2.1% 2023-24; now rising on inflation/interest-driven allowed returns, transmission build, under-recovery; overhead transmission costs +25–55% real vs 2024 estimates.
3. Retail [H]: 7–16% of DMO; AER allows 6% margin, ACCC finds actual NSW margins 0–4% since 2019-20 → not a driver. DMO: 2023-24 ≈+20–25% [mem]; 2025-26 Ausgrid $1,965 (+8.6%), Energex $2,143 (+3.7%), SAPN $2,301 (+3.2%); 2026-27 Ausgrid $1,899 (−3.4%), Energex $1,988 (−7.2%), SAPN $2,334 (+1.4%).
4. Policy [H]: Energy Bill Relief ≈$1.5b (2023-24, targeted [mem]), $3.5b (2024-25, $300 all households), $1.8b (2025-26, $150 Jul–Dec) ≈ $6.8b total. Gas cap $12/GJ from 23 Dec 2022, ongoing via Mandatory Code Jul-2023; NSW coal cap $125/t held coal gen ≈$49/MWh through 2023–H1 2024.
5. Gas: three QLD LNG plants (from 2015) tied east coast to export parity; contracts $13–15/GJ (above cap); Gippsland decline (Bass Strait ≈⅔ of southern supply); pipeline limits into VIC; moratoria VIC/NSW/NT [mem]; east coast now $10.77/GJ (Q3 2026), below LNG spot; 20% export reservation on new contracts from 2027 [M].
6. What households paid (Ausgrid DMO net of rebates, calc): $1,510 (2024-25) → $1,815 (2025-26) → $1,899 (2026-27) = +26% in two years though DMO fell; Energex net ≈$766 (2024-25, incl. $1,000 state rebate) vs $1,988 (2026-27).
7. Forward: AEMC per-unit prices −5% to 2030, then +13% 2030–35 if build runs late; GenCost $80–90/MWh to 2030, after 2030 no new-build <$100/MWh incl. coal; 2050 net-zero system $141–152/MWh incl. transmission; ISP optimal plan $106b PV to 2050 (ex $48b committed, ex household solar/batteries), transmission net benefit $28b; Coalition nuclear $331b vs $594b used lower-demand scenario; Frontier's Step Change w/ nuclear $115b higher [M].

## C. Verdict
Energy — unavoidable: 2022 fuel shock, coal ageing out. Chosen: LNG exports without domestic reservation (root of gas +44%; reservation ≈10 yrs late); state reliability rules inflating RAB 2006–13. Poor value: ≈$6.8b mostly untargeted rebates deferred bills (Ausgrid net bill +26% as they ended); transmission delays (+25–55%).
Spending — demographics+price explain ≈90% Medicare, ≈70% hospitals, ≈55% PBS, ≈half aged care, ≈35% childcare, 0% defence. Chosen: aged care/childcare wage funding (defensible post-RC/court); $25b hospital uplift; $8.5b bulk billing; defence/AUKUS; structural deficits → interest. Weakest value: bulk billing ($859m/yr patient savings by 2030 vs ≈$2b/yr cost; rest to practices [M]); hospital money without activity/outcome conditions [M]; back-loaded defence profile [M].
