# Research brief: bracket creep, 2026 tax package, housing (agent output, 2026-10-26)

## 0. CGT / negative gearing — Treasury Laws Amendment (Tax Reform No. 1) Act 2026 (No. 49, assented 26 Jun 2026)
- CGT: from 1 Jul 2027 the 50% discount is replaced by cost-base indexation + 30% minimum tax on real gains (individuals, trusts, partnerships; not companies/super). Gains before 1 Jul 2027 keep the discount (deemed sale/reacquisition). New residential dwellings/affordable housing may choose either. Income-support recipients exempt from the minimum tax.
- Negative gearing: NOT per property — net losses across all quarantined established residential properties not deductible against wages; offset against residential rental income/capital gains, excess carried forward. From 2027-28 income year, for established dwellings acquired after 7:30pm 12 May 2026. Exempt: earlier holdings, new dwellings (definition by instrument), BTR, widely-held trusts, super. Senate amendment: ban on SMSF limited-recourse borrowing.
- Costings (receipts $bn): NG/CGT 2028-29 +1.35, 2029-30 +2.28 ($3.6bn/5yr); trust minimum tax 2029-30 +4.47; WATO 2028-29 −3.02, 2029-30 −3.36 (−$6.4bn). PBO prelim: just over $40bn from NG/CGT over 2026-27→2036-37, and about the same from the trust tax (MTBO Jul 2026 p.32).
- Coalition pledged repeal.

## 1. Bracket creep
- In force: 16%→15% from 1 Jul 2026, →14% from 1 Jul 2027 (BP1 p.13). Stage 3 from Jul 2024. $250 Working Australians Tax Offset (WATO) from 2027-28 lifts effective tax-free threshold to $19,985. $1,000 instant deduction from 2026-27.
- Average rate (Treasury BP1 p.29): all taxpayers 25.5% (2023-24) → 24.7% (2027-28); average earner 21.9% → 20.2%, back above 2023-24 level by 2032-33.
- Average rate (PBO MTBO, aggregate incl. non-taxable, offsets, Medicare): 24.9% (2026-27) → 28.6% (2036-37), a record (historical range 22–25% over ~40 yrs). Personal tax 12.6% → 14.7% of GDP; 47.9% → 53.8% of revenue. UCB −1.0% GDP → surplus 2034-35 "mainly bracket creep"; PBO calls no-further-cuts assumption "unrealistic". Don't splice the two series (different definitions).
- Individuals & withholding receipts: $364.2bn (2025-26), 382.4, 399.0, 419.6, 450.3 (2029-30) (BP1 T5.7). No official bracket-creep-only dollar figure; Taylor's ">$20bn over 4 yrs" unverified.
- Indexation costings: Coalition "Tax Back Guarantee" (index bottom two thresholds from 2028-29, top two from 2031-32): Coalition says $22.5bn/4yr (PBO BYOB); ABC via PBO SMART $35.3bn (3.0, 6.4, 10.1, 15.8), $44.5bn in 2035-36. No formal PBO costing. PBO: full CPI indexation keeps budget out of surplus over medium term.
- Positions: Labor — new fiscal objective to "lower the tax burden on working Australians over time". Coalition — indexation + repeal NG/CGT. Greens — voted for NG/CGT.
