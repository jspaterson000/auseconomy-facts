# Research brief: starting a business as a young person, 2026-27 settings (agent output, 2026-10-26)
Caveat: ATO/Treasury/Fair Work pages mostly blocked; figures from accounting-firm/aggregator pages. "Announced" = in 12 May 2026 Budget, not yet legislated.

## Personal tax
- 2025-26: 0% to $18,200; 16% to $45k; 30% to $135k; 37% to $190k; 45% above. 16%→15% from 1 Jul 2026 (legislated); →14% from 1 Jul 2027 (reported legislated; confirm). LITO $700 to $37.5k, phasing out to nil at $66,667. Medicare 2%, singles low-income threshold $28,011.
- Announced: $1,000 no-receipts work-expense deduction from 1 Jul 2026 (employees; reportedly not sole traders); Working Australians Tax Offset up to $250 from 2027-28 (wage and sole-trader income).

## Business settings
- Instant asset write-off $20k permanent from 1 Jul 2026, turnover <$10m, not indexed (announced). Loss carry-back returns from 1 Jul 2026 for companies <$1b, two years back, capped by franking balance (announced). Start-up loss refund from 1 Jul 2028, first two years, <$10m turnover, capped at PAYG withholding + FBT paid — little use without staff (announced). R&D (from 1 Jul 2028): min spend $20k→$50k, intensity threshold 2%→1.5%, refundability needs turnover <$50m and company ≤10 yrs old. ESVCLP cap $50m→$80m from 1 Jul 2027. Discretionary trusts 30% minimum tax from 1 Jul 2028, 3-yr rollover relief to 30 Jun 2030 (exposure draft Sep 2026). Small business CGT concessions (Div 152) preserved. GIC/SIC non-deductible from 1 Jul 2025 (Royal Assent 27 Mar 2025). Div 7A: no change; rate unconfirmed. Base rate entity 25% (<$50m, ≤80% passive), GST threshold $75k, PSI rules: from background knowledge, not re-verified. ESIC: reported intact; founder equity keeps no special CGT treatment (Budget promised consultation).

## Employing
- SG 12% since 1 Jul 2025; payday super from 1 Jul 2026 (7 business days; ATO clearing house closed). Minimum wage $26.44/hr ($1,004.90/wk) from 1 Jul 2026 (rise % conflicting: 4.75% vs 5.97%). Non-compete ban: not law; draft, possible start 1 Jan 2027, threshold $190,100 (DLA Piper) vs older $175k. Payroll tax: NSW $1.2m @5.45%; VIC $1m @4.85%; QLD $1.3m @4.75% (aggregator). Unfair dismissal small-business rules (<15 employees, 12-month qualifying period) unverified.

## Founder's personal position
- HELP: 20% cut legislated (balances at 1 Jun 2025); marginal repayment: nil to $69,528 (2026-27), then 15c/$ to $129,717. Div 296 irrelevant at modest balances. 5% deposit scheme: income caps and place limits removed 1 Oct 2025.
- Effective rates 2026-27 (scale + 2% Medicare, LITO; no SBITO):

Fully distributed company = sole trader (imputation). Retained 25% is deferral only. Small business income tax offset up to $1,000 for sole traders (unchecked).

## Contested / traps
- Grandfathered NG/CGT changes favour incumbents (ABC, Grattan); founders' share exits and ESS lose the CGT discount from 1 Jul 2027 (no founder carve-out).
- Threshold cliffs: $10m (write-off, start-up refund), $50m (BRE), $1b (carry-back); $75k GST; 15-employee dismissal.
- Trusts face 30% from 2028; restructuring triggers stamp duty rollover doesn't cover → companies become default vehicle. Carry-back/start-up refunds capped by franking/wages → little for new founders. Payday super makes quarterly payment late. Tax-debt interest non-deductible. Several measures announced only.
