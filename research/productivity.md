# Research brief: productivity (agent output, 2026-10-26)

Key points: the slowdown vs 2004–14 is almost entirely capital shallowing (MFP held up); Treasury (2026 IGR) assumes 1.2% trend productivity, RBA 0.7%; reforms are being delivered but measured productivity is flat.

## 1. Numbers
- GDP per hour worked (calc from ABS ANA_AGG M5.GPM_PHW): 1980s 1.20% p.a.; 1990s 2.25%; 2000s 1.43%; 2010s 1.13%; 2014-15→2024-25 0.27%; FY2024-25 −0.55%, FY2025-26 +0.58%; through the year to Jun-2026 −0.2% (output +2.1%, hours +2.3%; PC update 3 Sep 2026 https://www.pc.gov.au/ongoing/productivity-insights/update-september-2026).
- Market sector GVA per hour: 2000s 1.81%, 2010s 1.55%, last 10 yrs 0.55%; FY25 −0.25%, FY26 +0.83%.
- Since Jun-2014: market sector +7.5%, non-market −2.5%, whole economy +3.6%; non-market productivity below its Mar-2007 level (PC, Sep 2026).
- MFP 2024-25 (ABS 5260.0.55.002, 6 Feb 2026): market MFP −0.5%, LP −0.2%; quality-adjusted MFP −0.8%, LP −0.7%; mining MFP −3.3%.
- Market-sector decomposition (log % p.a., LP = MFP + capital deepening):
  | Period | LP | MFP | Capital deepening |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1994-95→2004-05 | 2.50 | 1.31 | 1.19 |
  | 2004-05→2014-15 | 1.85 | 0.29 | 1.56 |
  | 2014-15→2024-25 | 0.57 | 0.39 | 0.19 |
  | 2021-22→2024-25 | −0.92 | −0.44 | −0.48 |
  Mining MFP −3.5% p.a. in 2004–14 held that decade's MFP down.

## 2. Diagnoses
- Capital shallowing: largest term; RBA (Senate submission Feb 2026) calls it "a symptom as well as a cause". Non-mining capex up sharply to Jun-2026, mostly data centres (CEDA 2026).
- Non-market (care) sector: drag ≈0.3 ppt p.a. 2017/18–2023/24 vs 0.15 before (RBA SMP Aug 2025). Contested: output measured from inputs (PC Dec 2025).
- Mining: productivity −20% over ~5 yrs (lower grades); RBA treats mining + non-market as temporary, about half its expected rebound.
- Energy transition: utilities MFP −1.47% p.a. 2014–24 (new assets ramping; PC Jun 2026).
- Housing construction: construction MFP −1.70% p.a. 2014–24; over 30 yrs GVA/hour −12%, dwellings completed per hour −53% (PC Feb 2025). No quantified zoning-to-aggregate-productivity estimate found.
- Competition/dynamism: RBA, 2026 IGR, OECD (Jan 2026) cite rising concentration/margins; new-firm share of employing businesses 13%→11% (2005-06→2018-19). King (2023): explains only part.
- Regulation: claimed $10.2b p.a. red-tape cut is self-reported.
- Skills/migration: RBA — less-skilled entry explains "only a small share"; ABS labour composition ADDED 0.31 ppt p.a. 2017–22. Undercuts "migration dilutes skills".
- AI: PC interim +4.3% LP over a decade (uncertain); two-thirds of firms use AI, <10% call it significant (Chalmers 21 Sep 2026).
- Trend assumptions: RBA 0.7% (Aug 2025, 20-yr average); 2026 IGR 1.2% (reportedly 30-yr look-back).

## 3. 2025 reform agenda status
- Roundtable Aug 2025: 10 directions; Treasurer claims ~60 reforms + ~60 in a Regulatory Reform Omnibus Bill (19 Aug 2026; self-reported).
- EPBC reforms passed Senate 27–28 Nov 2025; national environmental regulator from 1 Jul 2026.
- PC final reports Dec 2025 (47 recs). Budget 12 May 2026 claims "13 of 17" PC reform areas progressed.
- PC 20%/28% company tax + 5% cash-flow tax NOT adopted; Budget made instant asset write-off permanent, added loss carry-back and start-up loss refundability (Corrs).
- **CGT and negative gearing changes passed Parliament 25 Jun 2026** (Capital Brief) — details to confirm.
- No measured payoff yet.
