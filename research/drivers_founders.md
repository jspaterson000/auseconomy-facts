# Founders, startups and capital offshore: exodus or anecdotes? (agent output, 2026-10-26)
Short answer: visible anecdotes, not a measured exodus. Market size and US capital explain most corporate moves; tax is the one reason a named founder gave, and the 2026 CGT change has since been largely softened by a draft carve-out.

## 1. Cases
- Fred Schebesta (Finder): flew to Singapore week of 8 Oct 2026, "staged" family move, ATO residency undisclosed; called CGT overhaul "nail in the coffin… the innovators are leaving"; cited Singapore zero CGT, proximity to China, welcoming govt [established he said it]. No Dubai report; no Restuccia move found. Finder (2006) too old for the new 15-year carve-out → his tax point is real for him.
- Canva: Feb-2025 new Delaware top company Canva, Inc.; old parent → Canva Australia Holdings; operations "substantially the same" (ATO CR 2025/35); HQ Sydney; no reason stated; no S-1 as of Oct-2026. What moved: legal top company only.
- Atlassian: 2022 redomicile was UK → Delaware (left Australia 2013–14); reasons: investor access, index inclusion, simpler structure, capital raising; HQ Sydney.
- Airwallex: HQ Melbourne → Hong Kong 2018; US HQ added Dec-2025 amid cut to Chinese shareholder; Lucy Liu: "Australia is quite small", talent pipeline "will restrict you"; no tax reason.
- Rokt: HQ New York; Jan-2024 expansion with up to US$4.7m NY state tax credits (a tax incentive pulled it to the US).
- Culture Amp, Linktree (Melbourne), Immutable (Sydney): no relocation evidence; SafetyCulture, Go1, Employment Hero not verified.

## 2. Aggregate evidence
- ABS: Australian-citizen departures 81.5k (2024-25), down from 85.8k, below 2014–19 avg 93k (all citizens).
- Cut Through Venture: 2025 raised A$5.1–5.4b (+24–31%); deal count −17% (470→390); 66% of deals had an international investor (57% prior). Does not measure relocations.
- Henley millionaire migration: 2025 projected Australia +1,000 (net inflow, lowest on record), Singapore +1,600, UAE +9,800 [claimed]; accused of failing forensic tests (FT/Tax Justice Network); 2026 report dropped New World Wealth and country counts → unreliable.
- Tech Council 2026 releases: no founder-departure data; advisers: "several" redomiciles a year [anecdote].
- Not measured: ATO HWI departures; Singapore EDB counts; LinkedIn/academic founder-emigration studies.

## 3. Drivers ranked by evidence
1. Market size and access to US capital/customers (filings and founders' own words) [strongest].
2. Capital depth: fewer deals, reliance on offshore cheques [moderate].
3. CGT on exit: 50% discount → indexation + 30% minimum from 1 Jul 2027 (pre-2027 gains keep discount). Near-zero cost base, $5m exit: tax ≈$1.18m → $2.35m (23.5% → 47%). CORRECTION: proposed founder/ESS carve-out exists — Innovative Business CGT Concession exposure draft 11 Sep 2026 (consultation closed 28 Sep): keeps 50% discount with NO 30% minimum for qualifying firms: company <15 yrs, turnover ≤$50m, unlisted, innovation test, 3-yr holding, no lifetime cap; ESS via employee share trusts covered. Draft, not law. Leaving does not escape accrued gains: ceasing residency triggers deemed disposal; non-residents lose discount on later gains.
4. Personal income tax: 45% + 2% above $190k vs Singapore 24% above S$1m, UAE 0%; matters mainly post-exit (early founders draw small salaries).
5. Talent/visas: Liu's quote only.
6. R&D incentive: mixed, not simply tightening: core offset rises to ≈48% from Jul-2028; supporting activities removed; min claim $50k; $50m refundable threshold only for firms <10 yrs.
7. Div 296: passed Senate Mar-2026, from 1 Jul 2026; UNREALISED GAINS REMOVED, realised earnings only; +15% above $3m, +10% more above $10m; optional cost-base reset 30 Jun 2026; near-irrelevant for 25–35-year-olds.
8. ESS: 2022 reform removed cessation-of-employment taxing point (a fix). Payroll tax/state fragmentation: no evidence either way.

## 4. Counter-evidence
- Most "moves" are legal flips with staff and IP in Australia (Canva, Atlassian, flip guides).
- Singapore limits: Employment Pass floors S$5,600–10,700/month (S$6,000–11,500 from 2027), points test favouring local hiring.
- Peer tax: Australia's ≈23.5% exit tax already above US federal QSBS (§1202: 0% on gains to US$15m, 5-yr hold), Singapore/UAE 0%. Small-business CGT concessions unchanged but most VC-backed firms fail the $6m net-asset test; ESIC relief goes to investors. IBCC would roughly restore the pre-change rate, not beat peers.
- Inbound founder data: none found; international investor participation rising.

## 5. Verdict
Anecdotes: one named relocation (Schebesta); Bassat "will leave in big numbers" has no data; aggregate migration shows no exodus. For a 25–35-year-old what matters: IBCC final rules (innovation test, $50m cap), access to US capital/customers, personal tax only at large salaries. Cheap fixes: legislate IBCC with simple safe harbours; rollover relief so flips don't trigger phantom CGT; raise the $6m net-asset test; measure founder emigration (ATO residency-cessation data).
Gaps: Startup Genome, Dealroom, Singapore EDB, VC dry powder, super allocation to VC, Henley 2026 Australia figure, SafetyCulture/Go1/Employment Hero.
