# Measurement audit: "9 in 10 under-35s don't have shares" (agent output, 2026-10-26)

**Bottom line:** the claim concerned under-35s (Chalmers, ABC Insiders 17 May 2026, "about nine in 10 people under 35 don't have any shares", defending removal of the CGT discount on shares). It traces to a Treasury FOI slide: "11 per cent of Australians under 35 received income from shares", which "accounted for various forms of dividend income" (Yahoo Finance 24 Jul 2026). Conflicting secondary accounts on whether capital gains were included (Rampart 24 May 2026 says yes). The figure is a lower bound on *income received from shares*, not an estimate of *ownership*.

**What the proxy misses:** ETF distributions land at Item 13 (trusts) and foreign income at Item 20, not the dividend labels; ~$150bn in foreign-based ASX ETFs (Stockspot); non-dividend growth stocks unless sold; micro-investing apps (pooled trusts); crypto; holdings via family trusts/companies; non-lodgers (younger people lodge less); tiny amounts unreported.

**Triangulation:**
- ATO all-ages 2022-23: 3.05m with franked dividends; 2.12m with trust amounts (age tables not retrieved — TODO pull directly).
- ASX Australian Investor Study 2026 (online panel, n=4,495, Jan–Feb 2026): 11.5m of 21.4m adults hold investments outside super/home; 8.3m on-exchange; 18-29 = 21% of investors, 16% of non-investors → ≈60% of under-30s invest in something; if the 72% on-exchange share applies → ≈43% hold on-exchange assets (unverified assumption). 2023 study: among 18-24 investors 43% hold Aus shares, 33% ETFs, 31% crypto.
- ASIC/YouGov Dec 2025 (n=1,000, 18-28, self-reported): 18% own shares, 23% crypto, 8% commodities.
- Platform: CommSec ~3m customers, 43% under 40 (secondary); Stake 2025: 32% Gen Z / 36% millennials invest in shares (unchecked).
- ATO crypto data-matching 2019: 0.5–1m holders; ASX 2026 survey 2.79m.

**Verdict:** direct shares/ETFs ≈20–45% of under-30s; any investment outside super ≈60%; crypto ≈20–25%. The dividend proxy (≈11%) understates ownership by roughly 2–4x. Best source for level: ASX 2026 (survey caveats: opt-in panel over-represents engaged investors); ASIC survey as lower bound. Clean answer needs ATO age tables with Item 13/20 counts.
