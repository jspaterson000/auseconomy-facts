# Research brief: NDIS, welfare, redistribution (agent output, 2026-10-26)
Sources: BP1/BP2 2026-27 (12 May 2026), FBO 2025-26, MYEFO 2025-26, NDIA Q4 2025-26 report, NDIS AFSR June 2025, PBO MTBO 2026-27, TEIS 2025-26, NSW NDIS schedule. Extracted texts in scratchpad (bp1_2627.txt, bp2.txt, q4.txt, afsr.txt, mtbo.txt, teis.txt).

## NDIS
- Participant support costs (accrual): 2021-22 $27.9b; 22-23 $35.2b; 23-24 $41.9b; 24-25 $46.3b (AFSR T4.9); 2025-26 $51.2b, +10.5%, $492m above AFSR projection (NDIA Q4). PBO: avg growth 18.7% over 5 yrs.
- Budget estimates (Cth + states + dept program, BP1 T6.9.2): 2025-26 $53,778m; 26-27 $56,125m; 27-28 $55,082m; 28-29 $55,599m; 29-30 $56,239m — i.e. essentially flat nominal for 3 years after 10.5% growth.
- Beyond: govt assumes 5% growth from 2030-31. PBO MTBO: $72.0b in 2035-36 (was $106.7b previous outlook), $75.3b 2036-37 (1.5% GDP). Actuary pre-reform: $95.8b in 2034-35 (2.1% GDP). Treasury: Cth participant costs +3.6% p.a. 2026-27→2036-37 vs 7.6% at MYEFO. Basis differs (PBO $53.7b vs Budget $56.1b for 2026-27).
- Participants 782,013 at 30 Jun 2026. Avg payment +2.4% p.a. over 2 yrs; continuing participants $65,800→$69,900. Plan inflation 9.7%→4.7%.
- Growth target: 8% from 1 Jul 2026 superseded by National Cabinet target 5–6% or lower (BP1 St.9).
- "Securing the NDIS" (BP2): claimed $37.8b/4yr saving vs actuary projection (NDIA line −1.1, −7.3, −11.7, −16.5 $b); that projection had risen $13.9b, so net vs MYEFO $23.9b. Functional-capacity access from 1 Jan 2028; new framework planning from 1 Apr 2027. Grattan: $13.2b of saving = blunt cuts (−50% social/community participation budgets, −10% capacity building). Thriving Kids $4b/5yr shared with states from 1 Oct 2026; +$3b Cth foundational supports. Bill passed Senate ~19 Aug 2026.
- Contested: exits ~160k (ABC) vs 240k exits + 110k diverted by 2031; CBA: savings "very unlikely" in full; PBO 10%-growth scenario adds $68.1b in 2036-37.
- Cth vs states: NSW $4,120m 2025-26, +4% p.a. to 2027-28 then lower of actual growth or 8%; states ≈22–25% (agent estimate), Cth ≈75%. Of $184.9b savings to 2036-37 only $32.5b flows to states.

## Redistribution / tax
- Div 296: law since 13 Mar 2026, start 1 Jul 2026: 30% on earnings for balances >$3m, 40% >$10m, realised earnings, indexed thresholds; ~$1.6b net in 2028-29 (MYEFO).
- Tax Reform No.1 Bill passed 25 Jun 2026: from 1 Jul 2027 negative gearing limited to new builds (properties held at 12 May 2026 exempt); 50% CGT discount replaced by indexation + 30% minimum tax; +$3.6b over 5 yrs; Greens won ban on SMSF borrowing for residential property. "75,000 extra first-home owners" is Treasury's unaudited claim.
- Discretionary trusts: 30% minimum tax from 1 Jul 2028, +$4.5b over 5 yrs (BP2: receipts 2029-30 $4,470m; nil earlier), not legislated; exposure draft 7 Sep 2026. ~840k trusts distributed $142.4b in 2022-23; top 10% of households hold >90% of private trust value.
- Whole tax package improves UCB by $77.2b 2025-26→2036-37 (BP1).
- No major party proposes an inheritance tax.
- TEIS 2025-26 distribution (2022-23 data): CGT discount $21.8b, 83% to top decile (top 1% earn 54% of capital gains); super contributions $32.2b, 32% top decile; super earnings $28.5b, 38%. Negative gearing: top 10% get ~half of lifetime benefit. Caveat: TEIS CGT benchmark = nominal gains taxed in full (overstates vs real-gains benchmark). **Measurement audit: TEIS deciles are by taxable income.**
- Wealth: ABS SIH 2019-20 top quintile 63% of wealth, bottom quintile <1%; next release "unknown" (2023-24 survey possibly cancelled — unverified). PC 2021: ~$1.5t transferred over 2 decades, ~90% inheritances, 4x real by 2050; PC finds transfers reduce relative inequality (contested).
- Parties: Labor = package above. Coalition (Taylor, since 13 Feb 2026): repeal NG/CGT changes; index tax brackets ($22.5b/4yr, offsets unnamed); restrict NDIS to citizens. Greens: backed CGT changes, opposed NDIS bill; billionaire tax PBO-costed $106.8b to 2028-29.
