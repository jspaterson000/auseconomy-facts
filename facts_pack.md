# Key facts pack (curated from the model and research briefs; each has a primary source in reports/research or the database)
As at 27 Oct 2026. Use these as the first line of fact-checking. "Model" = auseconomy database.

## Living standards
- Real household disposable income per person: +4.5% vs 2019, −5.3% vs Sep-2021 peak (ABS national accounts; model `household_income`).
- Real wages (WPI − CPI) −0.7pp y/y Jun-2026; WPI +22.2% since 2019 vs CPI +28.0% → ≈−4.5% real for the same job (model).
- Living costs since 2019: employee households +31%, age pensioners +29%, CPI +28% (ABS SLCI).
- Median net worth 55-64 vs 25-34 households: 5.7x in 2019-20 ($1.00m vs $176k); no ABS wealth survey since (ABS SIH).
- Unemployment 4.4–4.6%; underutilisation 10.9% (13.5% in 2019).

## Inflation
- Headline CPI 3.9% y/y Jun-q 2026 (4.0% Aug monthly); trimmed mean 3.6%; cash rate 4.60% after four 2026 rises (3.60 → 4.60; latest 29 Sep 2026, RBA F1 daily). Sep-q CPI due 28 Oct 2026.
- 2021–23 surge: RBA decompositions → supply ≈½–¾, demand the rest; without supply shocks underlying would still have been 3.1–3.5% (RBA Bulletin Jun 2023). Murphy (ANU): policy stimulus added 3.0pp to 2022 inflation (2.4 fiscal, 0.6 monetary). COVID support $429b; households received ≈$2 per $1 of income lost; excess saving ≈$300b.
- Profits: non-mining margins +0.4pp Dec-2019→Dec-2022 while CPI +12.6% (Treasury); RBA: not an independent driver.
- 2025–26 re-acceleration (+1.8pp headline): ≈0.9pp underlying, ≈0.5pp electricity rebate unwind, rest fuel/conflict (RBA SMPs).
- Model: no Commonwealth spending channel (transfers, consumption, grants, total, fiscal impulse) has a robust link to underlying inflation once 2020-21 is excluded; public demand 28.9% of GDP (record).

## Budget
- 2025-26 FBO: UCB −$22.3b (−0.8% GDP); payments 26.8% of GDP (highest ex-COVID since 1986); tax receipts 24.0% of GDP.
- Forward estimates: deficits ≈1% of GDP to 2029-30. Pre-COVID spending forecasts unbiased (+2.1% 1-yr ahead, n.s.); post-2020 outcomes beat forecasts by 10% (1 yr), 13% (2 yrs), 17% (3 yrs) (PBO vintages; model `forecast_error_summary`).
- Since the 2023-24 Budget, $143b added to 2023-24→2026-27 payments: $73.6b policy decisions (52%), $69.3b parameter variations (48%) (model `reconciliation`).
- Combined Cth + state net debt $920b (31% GDP, 2025-26) → $1,279b (36.5%) by 2029-30; QLD fastest ($15b→$98b) (PBO NFO).
- Cth net debt end-2025-26 $550.0b = 18.8% of GDP (down from 19.2% in 2024-25; 2022 PEFO expected 33.1%); gross debt $971.4b = 33.2% (FBO 2025-26 Table 1.1). Budget path: net debt 22.2% of GDP by 2029-30.
- Structural deficit 1.6% of GDP 2025-26 (Treasury). PBO: surplus 2034-35 "mainly bracket creep"; average tax rate to record 28.6% by 2036-37.
- Public debt interest $17b (2019-20) → $28b (2025-26) → $47b (2029-30).

## Tax
- Bracket creep since 2024-25 (WPI-indexed counterfactual): $34.3b/yr by 2029-30 (≈$1,900 per taxpayer), $26.9b on CPI benchmark; heaviest as % of income in deciles 4–5 ($44k–$73k) at 3.1–3.3% vs 1.7% top decile (model microsimulation on ATO 2023-24; matches PBO no-policy creep rate 0.42 vs 0.40pp/yr).
- Legislated: 16%→15% from 1 Jul 2026, →14% from 1 Jul 2027; $250 WATO from 2027-28. Coalition "Tax Back Guarantee" (index brackets, scrap the $250 WATO; ≈$250 in yr 1 rising to ≈$1,000/yr by yr 4; top two brackets indexed from 2031-32): May 2026 claim $22.5b/4yr vs $35.3b (ABC/PBO SMART tool). Updated costing 26 Oct 2026: PBO puts the tax plan at a $9.7b net cost, offset by a $31.3b savings package 2028-29→2031-32 (incl. $7.9b from cutting tobacco excise and legalising vapes, $4.7b housing, $2b removing welfare/NDIS eligibility for non-citizens). Different scope from the May figures, so don't compare directly; excludes the cost of migration cuts and of reversing the 2026 tax package (ABC, Liberal release 26 Oct 2026). [contested]
- 2026 package (Tax Reform No.1 Act, 26 Jun 2026): CGT 50% discount → indexation + 30% minimum tax on real gains from 1 Jul 2027 (pre-2027 gains keep discount; new-build investors may opt); negative gearing limited to new builds for established properties bought after 12 May 2026; +$3.6b/5yr (PBO ≈$40b/10yr). Div 296 law (Mar 2026): 30%/40% on *realised* super earnings above $3m/$10m from 1 Jul 2026. Trust 30% minimum tax from 1 Jul 2028 (+$4.47b in 2029-30; draft only). Draft Innovative Business CGT Concession (Sep 2026): keeps 50% discount, no minimum, for companies <15 yrs, ≤$50m turnover.
- TEIS: CGT discount 83% to top taxable-income decile; super contributions 32%; super earnings 38% (taxable-income basis caveat).
- Young investor, 32% rate, 10 yrs: effective tax on share returns ≈19% (old) → ≈23% (new); own home 0% with 10–20x leverage (model calc).

## NDIS / disability
- Participants 782,013 (Jun-2026); supports $51.2b (+10.5%); 18.7% avg growth over 5 yrs (PBO). PC 2011 design: 411k, $13.6b (≈1% GDP); 2017 projection for 2025-26 $34.8b → ≈$16b above plan.
- Autism 45% of participants, ≈79% of net growth since 2023; 11% of 6-year-olds; children drive headcount not dollars (non-SIL autism ≈$28k vs $67.5k avg); SIL 4.7% of participants = $16.6b.
- Leakage 8.2–8.3% of claims (≈$3.7b), "likely serious offending" ≈1.3% (NDIA to JSC, 2026); ANAO: pre-2024 prevention reviewed 0.4% of dollars, cancelled >50% of reviewed.
- Reforms: Act passed 19 Aug 2026; from 1 Oct 2026 social/community participation −50%, daily living −10%; participants projected 783k→632k by 2030 (895k no reform); $37.8b/4yr vs actuary path, $23.9b vs MYEFO; integrity+pricing only ≈$3.7b of it; Budget assumes ≈2% growth; PBO 10% scenario adds $68.1b by 2036-37 and prevents surplus. States fixed 4% to 2027-28; Cth share ≈76%.
- Disability sub-function (NDIS+DSP+carers) $92.9b 2025-26 = 11.4% of expenses. Budget NDIS program line (Cth+states) $56.1b 2026-27.
